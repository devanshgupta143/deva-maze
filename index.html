Rules of Ascension

Read carefully before you begin
  • Find the glowing yellow Modak on each level to reach the goal.
  • Touching a pink trap or a decoy item ends your run instantly and resets you to the start.
  • Not everything is what it seems — some walls are fake walls you can walk through, and some open floors are fake floors that block you.
  • Press the ACT button (or Space/E on keyboard) to use special abilities like flight, dropping barriers, or placing items.
  • Your score drops the longer you take and the more you die — move with purpose.
  • Use the ? HINT button anytime if you're stuck.
